### Skill Learning
- **writer** (v1.0.2) - Fix AI writing patterns that create repetitive and robotic content
  - Purpose: Eliminate robotic AI writing patterns and create engaging, human-like content
  - Key writing traps to fix:
    - **Paragraph Opener Trap:** Scan first words - 3+ paragraphs with "This/The/It" = robotic. Vary with action verbs, specific nouns
    - **Rhythm Monotony Trap:** Mix short punches with long flows. Use strategic fragments for emphasis and pace
    - **Vague Claim Trap:** Replace adjectives with numbers - "significant growth" → "40% growth". Delete weasel words
    - **List Overuse Trap:** Use prose for relationships/arguments, bullets only for instructions/features
    - **Parallel Structure Breaks:** Match verb forms and sentence patterns
    - **Transition Word Crutches:** Avoid "Furthermore, Moreover, In conclusion". Use echo technique instead
    - **Voice Drift in Long Documents:** Check tone every 200 words, pick 3-5 key phrases consistently
    - **Word Economy Traps:** "in order to" → "to", "due to the fact that" → "because". 80% rule
    - **Nominalization Trap:** Use verbs not nouns - "implementation of" → "implement", "decision-making" → "decide"
  - Quick tests:
    - Read aloud: monotone rhythm = boring
    - Flow test: if bullets feel choppy, try connected sentences
    - Zombie noun test: -tion, -ment, -ness endings often hide better verbs
  - When to use:
    - Writing long-form content (articles, reports, emails)
    - Reviewing AI-generated text for robotic patterns
    - Improving readability and engagement
    - Polishing final drafts before publishing
